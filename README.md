# Speeltuin

A place for small experiments and side projects. Each project lives in its own folder and runs on its own.

| Project | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [lightstrings](lightstrings/) | An air harp made of light: swipe through glowing strings to pluck them, or play with your hands via webcam. ([play](https://guyonvandenbos.github.io/speeltuin/lightstrings/)) |
| [vr-instrumenten](vr-instrumenten/PLAN.md) | Plan: a VR game to play guitar, piano, drums and more with your hands (Quest, WebXR). |
| [vr-instrumenten/lichtsnaren](vr-instrumenten/lichtsnaren/) | Step 1: Lightstrings in VR, with 3D hand tracking on Meta Quest. ([play](https://guyonvandenbos.github.io/speeltuin/vr-instrumenten/lichtsnaren/)) |
| [vr-instrumenten/gitaar](vr-instrumenten/gitaar/) | Step 2: a guitar in VR. Fretting hand picks chords on the neck, the other hand strums. ([play](https://guyonvandenbos.github.io/speeltuin/vr-instrumenten/gitaar/)) |
